Art of Watchmaking

The Rolex Way

Excellence without compromise. Every Rolex watch is an alliance of sublime aesthetics and state-of-the-art technology.

Oyster Architecture

Invented by Rolex in 1926, the Oyster case is guaranteed waterproof to a depth of at least 100 meters, protecting the movement from water, dust, and shocks.

Perpetual Movement

Self-winding mechanical movements certified as Superlative Chronometers. Featuring the patented Parachrom hairspring offering 10 times greater precision against shocks.

Cerachrom Ceramic

Virtually scratchproof and impervious to ultraviolet rays. Diamond-polished ceramic bezels with numerals coated in yellow gold or platinum via PVD deposition.

Pioneering History

A Tradition of Perpetual Discovery

Hans Wilsdorf founded Rolex with the vision that a wristwatch could be both elegant and extraordinarily reliable. From the highest peaks of the Himalayas to the deepest depths of the Mariana Trench, Rolex watches accompany humanity's greatest achievements.
